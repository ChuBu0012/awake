# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Awake welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help keep participation respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Maintainer response

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate it and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

Report a conduct concern privately through the contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ChuBu0012). Please include links or context needed to understand the concern. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, and discussions, and when someone is representing the project in public.
